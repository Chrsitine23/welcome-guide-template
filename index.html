Key Location

The key is stored in a key box inside Mailbox 5D, Cluster 1.

How to Find the Key

  1. Go to Lobby Concierge
    From the lobby concierge counter, head straight toward the left side. As you continue walking, you will see the hallway on your right side.

    Cluster 2 will be on your left.
  2. Go to the Mailbox Room
    Walk straight down the hallway. At the end, you will see the mailbox room.
  3. Find Mailbox 5D
    Please locate Mailbox 5D and retrieve the key from the keybox.

    The mailbox and keybox can be opened directly and do not require any code or key.
  4. Proceed to the 5th floor
    Go outside the mailbox room where you will find two elevators. You can take either one to go up to the 5th floor.

Option 1

  • Use the right elevator and go up to the 5th floor.
  • Turn right, then turn right again.
  • Proceed to Room 5D.

Option 2

  • Use the left elevator and go up to the 5th floor.
  • Turn left, then turn right.
  • Proceed to Room 5D.
Note:
You may obtain your pool stub from the concierge during or after check-in.

The pool is located on the 5th floor.

Pool Hours

8:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Monday Alternative – Beach

When the building pool is closed on Mondays, guests may visit Mactan Newtown Beach for the discounted short-term guest rate of ₱350 per person.

Children below 6 years old are free, with a limit of 4 guests.

Beach Visiting Hours

Condominium guests may access the beach from 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.

Access after 5:00 PM requires payment of the applicable Night Pass fee.

Beach Entrance

To get the discounted rate, proceed to the Mactan Newtown Beach Welcome Cabana Reception Area and inform the staff that you are staying at:

8 Newtown Boulevard, Cluster 1, Room 5D

After payment, you will receive a wristband for entry.

Corkage fees may apply for outside food and beverages.

Walking Distance

Mactan Newtown Beach is approximately 10–15 minutes walking distance from the building.

Regular beach entrance rates for non-guests are ₱1,300 weekdays, ₱1,500 weekends (6:00 AM–6:00 PM), and ₱850 for the Night Pass (6:00 PM–10:00 PM). Rates may change without prior notice. Regular entrance fees include a buffet meal.

Activities

Things to Do Around Mactan Newtown

  • 🏖️ Mactan Newtown Beach

    Enjoy a relaxing day by the beach, swim, take photos, or simply unwind by the sea. The beach is located within Mactan Newtown and is approximately 10–15 minutes on foot from the building.

  • 🌴 Mactan Newtown Boardwalk

    Take a relaxing walk around the township and enjoy the seaside views, landscaped streets, restaurants, cafés, and the laid-back atmosphere of Mactan Newtown.

  • 🍽️ Dining & Food Trips

    Explore the restaurants, cafés, and food spots around Mactan Newtown. Enjoy Filipino, Korean, Western, and international dishes within walking distance.

  • ☕ Cafés & Chill Spots

    Grab coffee, enjoy dessert, work remotely, or relax at one of the many cafés around Mactan Newtown.

Nearby Mactan Attractions

  • ⚔️ Mactan Shrine

    Visit the historic Mactan Shrine and see the Lapu-Lapu Monument, Magellan Marker, and Battle of Mactan landmarks. A great place to learn more about Cebu's history.

  • 🏛️ Mactan World Museum

    Discover Filipino and Spanish heritage through exhibits, historical displays, and stories about Lapu-Lapu and the Battle of Mactan.

  • 🤿 Island Hopping & Snorkeling

    Explore the waters around Mactan and nearby islands. Enjoy snorkeling, swimming, diving, and island-hopping tours available around Mactan.

  • 🌊 Water Sports

    Try different water activities around Mactan Island, including snorkeling, diving, and other ocean adventures.

Relax & Enjoy Mactan

  • 💆 Spa & Massage

    Relax after a day of exploring with a massage or spa treatment. Several spas are located within a short distance of Mactan Newtown.

  • 🛍️ Shopping & Essentials

    Explore the shops and retail areas around Mactan Newtown for snacks, essentials, souvenirs, and everyday needs.

  • 🌅 Sunset Walk

    Take an evening walk around the Newtown waterfront and enjoy the sunset and relaxing seaside atmosphere.

Island Adventures

  • 🏝️ Olango Island

    Take a boat trip to Olango Island and explore its natural surroundings and wildlife sanctuary.

  • 🏖️ Maribago Beaches

    Explore the beaches and resort areas around Maribago, just a short drive from Mactan Newtown.

  • 🤿 Diving & Marine Adventures

    Mactan is a popular starting point for diving, snorkeling, and marine adventures around Cebu's coastline.

Power Outage

⚡ Power Outage Reminder

To help protect the air conditioner, water heater, refrigerator, and other appliances from power surges or electrical instability, please follow the instructions below during a power outage.

During a Power Outage

Turn OFF the MAIN BREAKER.

This helps protect the appliances from possible power surges or electrical instability when electricity is interrupted.

When Power Returns

1. Wait 3–5 minutes.
Allow the electricity to stabilize.

2. Turn ON the MAIN BREAKER.

Important Reminder

Some appliances, including the air conditioner, water heater, and refrigerator, may malfunction after a power outage.

Please make sure the MAIN BREAKER is turned OFF during an outage and only turn it back ON after waiting 3–5 minutes once power has returned.

Thank you for helping us protect the appliances and prevent inconvenience during power interruptions.
